AI Appropriateness Quest

Human-Centered, AI-Guided R Learning

This short decision game asks you to practice an intellectual value: keeping your own judgment at the center when AI is available. You will read realistic Intro to R situations and decide whether AI would support learning, weaken learning, or depend on how it is used.

About the feedback meters: after you choose a learner profile, five numbers will appear: Understanding, Tenacity, AI Judgment, Reproducibility, and Delegation Risk. They are not grades. They are feedback signals that show how different choices affect learning, verification, and outsourcing.

Decision rhythm for each scenario

  1. Attempt: try the task first, run code, read errors, and notice what happened.
  2. Document: keep evidence of the code, error, what you tried, and what changed.
  3. Ask: use AI for help that preserves your accountability, such as hints, diagnostic questions, or explanations.
  4. Verify: rerun, compare, and explain the result in your own words.
